Capabilities

Everything You Love About Personal Knowledge Bases

Crafted without compromises. Full parity with your markdown workflow.

CodeMirror 6 Live Preview

WYSIWYG markdown parsing rendered incrementally. Headers, bold, italics, strikethrough, callout blocks, math ($math$), and interactive checklists.

Wikilinks & Atomic Refactoring

Type [[ for zero-latency fuzzy title suggestions. Renaming a note atomically updates every reference across your entire vault in native Rust.

2D Graph with 0% Idle CPU

Force-directed canvas physics with spatial partitioning. Once nodes reach equilibrium, simulation loops automatically halt so your CPU stays at 0%.

Infinite Visual Canvas

An infinite 2D canvas for cards, notes, and ideas. Drag connection handles to route Bezier arrows. Fully compatible with Obsidian .canvas format.

Instant Quick Switcher

Zero-latency fuzzy search across note titles and content with Ctrl+O. Press Ctrl+P to execute commands, open daily notes, or toggle views.

100% Local & Freedom Protected

Zero cloud servers, telemetry, or tracking. Licensed under GPLv3, ensuring Shard and all future forks remain free, public, and open-source forever.
